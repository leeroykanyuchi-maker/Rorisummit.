01 / Automation system

OpsFlow

An enquiry triage prototype for service businesses. It validates incoming messages, classifies and extracts details, then applies readable rules to decide what can use a fixed template and what needs human approval. Every decision enters an audit log.

Why it matters: AI proposes; rules decide. Risky, incomplete or uncertain requests wait for a person.

02 / How I think

The interface matters.
So does the system behind it.

01

Start with the friction.

Understand the task, the person doing it and where the current process breaks before adding features.

02

Make decisions explainable.

For automation, use rules and review points people can understand. Record what happened and why.

03

Show the working thing.

A useful demo lets someone try the flow, find its weak spots and help shape the next version.
